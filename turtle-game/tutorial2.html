Your First Covered Call (Shares Only)

YOUR SETUP

You own: 100 shares of SPY at $600/share

Capital invested: $60,000

You sell: 1 call contract at $605 strike (7 days)

Premium collected: +$450

What Can Happen? (All 3 Scenarios)

SCENARIO 1: DOWN ↓

SPY: $600 → $595 (-$5)

Shares P&L: -$500

Call premium: +$450

Net P&L: -$50

💡 You lost money, but the premium softened the blow

SCENARIO 2: GOLDILOCKS ✓

SPY: $600 → $604 (+$4)

Shares P&L: +$400

Call premium: +$450

Net P&L: +$850 ✓✓

💡 You keep shares AND full premium. Best case!

SCENARIO 3: UP (Capped) ↑

SPY: $600 → $610 (+$10)

You must give up shares at $605!

Shares P&L: +$500

Call premium: +$450

Net P&L: +$950

But you missed $5 extra move! (Lost potential: $500)

Key Insight: You collect rent ($450/week), but cap your upside at the strike price.

When Price Crosses Your Strike

EXAMPLE: Getting Assigned

You bought shares at: $600

You sold $610 call for: $450 premium

Week ends at: $615

CALCULATION:

Share profit: ($610 - $600) × 100 = +$1,000

Call premium collected: +$450

Total profit: $1,450 ✓

But you gave up shares at $610!

If you kept shares: ($615 - $600) × 100 = $1,500

Opportunity cost: $50

Key Insight: You profit, but leave money on the table in big upward moves.

Rolling Your Option

KEEP SHARES BY ROLLING

Instead of giving up your shares, you can "buy back" the contract and sell another one.

Contract Value Over Time (Theta Decay) $450 │● │ ● │ ● │ ● │ ● │ ●● │ ●●● $0 │ ●●● └──────────────────── Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Theta decay accelerates near expiration!

ROLLING EXAMPLE:

Monday (Week 1): Sold call for +$450

Thursday (Week 1): Buy back call for -$100

Thursday (Week 1): Sell new call (Week 2) for +$420

Net for 2 weeks: $450 - $100 + $420 = $770

Result: You kept the shares! ✓

Key Insight: Rolling lets you keep collecting rent indefinitely without giving up shares.

Adding LEAPS (The Turtle Way)

Remember Tutorial 1? LEAPS with 85 delta function like 85 shares, but you "pay theta" (like interest on a mortgage).

Side-by-Side Comparison

COVERED SHARES

85 shares @ $600 = $51,000

Sell $610 call: +$450

Theta cost: $0

Weekly net: +$450

Monthly (4 weeks): +$1,800

Return over this period: 3.5%

COVERED LEAPS

1 LEAPS (85 delta) = $12,900

Freed capital: $38,100

Sell $610 call: +$450

Theta cost: -$14/week

Weekly net: +$436

Monthly (4 weeks): +$1,744

Return over this period: 13.5%

Notice: Same income (~$1,750/month), but LEAPS uses 75% less capital!

Quiz Time!

Q1: SPY goes from $600 → $605. Which gains more?

A) 85 shares
Share gain: 85 × $5 = +$425
B) 85-delta LEAPS
Delta gain: $5 × 0.85 × 100 = +$425, Theta: -$14 = +$411
Answer: A (85 shares)
Shares earn more because LEAPS has to pay the "theta cost" (-$14). However, LEAPS uses $38,100 less capital!

Q2: SPY stays flat at $600 for 4 weeks. Which performs better?

A) Covered shares
Share gain: $0, Call premium: 4 × $450 = +$1,800
B) Covered LEAPS
Delta gain: $0, Call premium: +$1,800, Theta: 4 × -$14 = +$1,744
Answer: A (Covered shares)
Shares win in flat markets because there's no theta cost. But the difference is only $56/month while using $38k less capital!

Q3: Which has better capital efficiency?

A) Covered shares
$1,800/month on $51,000 = 42% annual return
B) Covered LEAPS
$1,744/month on $12,900 = 162% annual return
Answer: B (Covered LEAPS)
Same income, way less capital! This is the Turtle Strategy: use LEAPS for capital efficiency, sell calls for income.
