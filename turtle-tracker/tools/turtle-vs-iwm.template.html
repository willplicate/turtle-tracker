    How the lines are built

    Turtle is every closed short call (sold minus bought back), plus realized gains on closed LEAPS, plus the open LEAPS mark minus what you paid. Open weekly calls are not counted until they close.

    Same capital in IWM takes the capital you had deployed at the start of each week and earns IWM's return for that week. Capital is Tastytrade cash from the export ($19,201 on Jan 1, plus the $4,000 deposit on Jun 24) and the tracker's IBKR allocation ($19,011 from Jan 26, $27,990 from Jun 23). It starts when each account bought its first LEAPS.

    Left out: commissions and fees, interest, and the QQQ trades. LEAPS marks come from your manual updates and carry forward between them. Weekly IWM closes are from Interactive Brokers.
