📋 Trading Rules Cheatsheet

🎯 LEAPS Selection Rules

  • Minimum DTE: Always buy LEAPS with at least 365 days to expiration
  • Roll Trigger: Roll position if DTE drops below 180 days
  • Delta Threshold: Roll if delta dips below 75% (0.75)

📉 Short Call Strategy (Volatility-Based)

Low Volatility
(VIX < 20)		 Sell OTM calls for premium
OR leave uncovered if in recovery mode
Medium Volatility
(VIX 20-30)		 Sell ATM calls for balanced risk/reward
High Volatility
(VIX > 30)		 Sell ITM calls for protection
Consider wider strikes if VIX spikes

💡 Quick Reminders

  • Check VIX before selling any call
  • LEAPS = Long-term exposure with less capital
  • Roll short calls before expiration if ITM and you want to keep the LEAPS
  • Keep cash reserve for defensive moves
